# Prime Global Candidate Agreement

Effective Date: 2026-07-23  
Last Updated: 2026-07-23  
Version: 0.1 (Draft)

## 1. Agreement Purpose

This Candidate Agreement describes terms applicable to individuals who create
profiles, submit applications, upload documents, or participate in recruitment
workflows through Prime Global.

## 2. Candidate Commitments

You agree to:

- Provide truthful and current professional information.
- Submit documents you are authorized to share.
- Respect communication standards with employers and staff.
- Avoid fraudulent identity, credential, or experience claims.

## 3. Profile And Application Processing

Your profile and application data may be used to:

- Match you with relevant opportunities.
- Support employer review and interview coordination.
- Maintain recruitment records and quality controls.

Submitting an application does not guarantee interview, offer, or placement.

## 4. Document Uploads

You are responsible for content and legality of uploaded materials, including
resumes, certifications, and identity documents when requested for compliance
or verification workflows.

## 5. AI-Assisted Evaluation

Prime Global may use AI-assisted tools for classification, extraction, and
matching support. Final recruitment outcomes require human oversight and may be
influenced by additional employer criteria.

## 6. Candidate Rights And Requests

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject
to legal retention requirements and legitimate operational constraints.

## 7. Platform Conduct

You must not:

- Use the platform for unlawful activity.
- Impersonate another person.
- Submit malicious files or harmful code.
- Harass or abuse other users.

## 8. Confidentiality And Employer Information

Materials shared by employers, including role details and interview content,
may be confidential. You must not disclose non-public information without
authorization.

## 9. Account Restrictions

Prime Global may suspend or close accounts involved in fraud, repeated policy
violations, or security risk.

outcomes, employer decisions, or uninterrupted service.

## 11. Governing Law

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]
