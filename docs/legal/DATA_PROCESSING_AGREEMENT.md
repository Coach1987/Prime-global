# Prime Global Data Processing Agreement

Effective Date: 2026-07-23  
Last Updated: 2026-07-23  
Version: 0.1 (Draft)

## 1. Parties And Applicability

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) applies when Prime Global processes
personal data on behalf of a customer acting as data controller in connection
with Prime Global services.

## 2. Subject Matter And Duration

Processing covers recruitment and related business support operations for the
contract term, including limited post-termination handling required for
deletion, return, legal compliance, and audit support.

## 3. Nature And Purpose Of Processing

Processing activities may include collection, storage, organization, retrieval,
analysis, transfer, and deletion of candidate, employer, and platform usage
data to deliver contracted services.

## 4. Categories Of Data Subjects

Data subjects may include candidates, employer representatives, platform
administrators, and support contacts.

## 5. Types Of Personal Data

Personal data may include identification data, contact data, professional
history, application records, communication metadata, and security logs.

## 6. Processor Obligations

Prime Global will:

- Process data only on documented controller instructions.
- Ensure personnel confidentiality commitments.
- Implement appropriate security measures.
- Assist with data subject rights where legally required.
- Notify controller of personal data breaches without undue delay.

## 7. Subprocessors

Prime Global may use approved subprocessors for hosting, storage,
communications, analytics, and security operations, subject to contractual
controls and due diligence.

## 8. International Transfers

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Cross-border transfers, when necessary, are governed by lawful transfer
mechanisms, such as contractual safeguards and supplementary controls aligned
with applicable legal standards.

## 9. Security Measures

Prime Global maintains risk-based security controls, including access
management, encryption practices where feasible, logging, monitoring, and
incident response procedures.

## 10. Audit And Information Rights

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Controller audit rights, frequency, confidentiality conditions, and cost
allocation should be defined in contract schedules to balance assurance and
operational security.

## 11. Deletion And Return

Upon termination and subject to legal obligations, Prime Global will delete or
return personal data in accordance with documented customer instructions and
technical feasibility constraints.

## 12. Liability

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Liability allocation under this DPA follows the governing commercial agreement,
except where non-waivable statutory obligations apply.
