# Prime Global Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-07-23  
Last Updated: 2026-07-23  
Version: 0.1 (Draft)

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Prime Global collects, uses, stores,
discloses, and protects personal data processed through our recruitment and
business support platform and related services.

## 2. Roles In Data Processing

Depending on the service context, Prime Global may act as:

- Data controller for website operations, support, and platform governance.
- Data processor on behalf of employer customers using our recruitment tools.

Where Prime Global acts as a processor, applicable terms are supplemented by
our Data Processing Agreement.

## 3. Personal Data We Process

We may process categories such as:

- Identity and contact information.
- Professional profile data, skills, work history, and education.
- Application materials, including resumes and attachments.
- Interview scheduling and communication metadata.
- Service usage and technical telemetry.
- Security and fraud signals.

We do not intentionally collect special category data unless required by law,
explicitly provided by users, or authorized under a valid legal basis.

## 4. Sources Of Personal Data

Data may come from:

- Candidates and employers directly.
- Public professional sources where legally permitted.
- Customer-provided integrations and workflow tools.
- Automated logs from platform usage.

## 5. Legal Bases

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

We rely on one or more legal bases, including contract necessity, legitimate
interests, consent (where required), and legal obligations. The exact basis may
vary by jurisdiction and processing purpose.

## 6. Purposes Of Processing

We process personal data to:

- Provide candidate and employer workflows.
- Match opportunities and support hiring decisions.
- Coordinate interviews and communications.
- Operate customer support and service administration.
- Secure systems, prevent abuse, and investigate incidents.
- Improve product quality and model performance with controls.
- Meet regulatory, legal, and audit obligations.

## 7. AI-Enabled Processing

We use AI-enabled components to assist ranking, categorization, extraction, and
operational recommendations. AI outputs are advisory and subject to human
oversight, especially where decisions may materially affect individuals.

## 8. Data Sharing

We may share personal data with:

- Employer customers and their authorized users.
- Service providers (hosting, storage, communication, analytics, security).
- Professional advisers and auditors under confidentiality controls.
- Authorities when legally required.
- Transaction counterparties during merger, acquisition, or restructuring.

We do not sell personal data in exchange for monetary consideration.

## 9. International Data Transfers

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Where data is transferred across borders, we implement appropriate safeguards,
which may include contractual clauses, transfer assessments, and technical
controls aligned with applicable law.

## 10. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide services, satisfy
legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce contracts, and maintain records.
Retention periods vary by data category and purpose.

## 11. Security

Prime Global applies organizational, technical, and procedural safeguards to
protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and unlawful
processing. See the Security Policy for additional controls.

## 12. Data Subject Rights

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, rectify,
erase, restrict processing, object, data portability, and withdraw consent.
Requests can be submitted via the contact channel below.

## 13. Children's Data

Our services are intended for professional and commercial use and are not
directed to children below the age threshold defined by local law.

## 14. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as legal, operational, or technical
requirements evolve. Material changes will be communicated through reasonable
channels.
