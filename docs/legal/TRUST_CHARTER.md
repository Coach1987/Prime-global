# Prime Global Trust Charter

Effective Date: 2026-07-23  
Last Updated: 2026-07-23  
Version: 0.1 (Draft)

## 1. Charter Purpose

The Prime Global Trust Charter defines the principles guiding how we build,
operate, and govern our platform for candidates, employers, partners, and
internal teams.

## 2. Trust Principles

### 2.1 Human-Centered Outcomes

We prioritize dignified, fair, and professional experiences for people using
our services.

### 2.2 Responsible Technology

We deploy automation and AI to assist, not replace, accountable human judgment
in high-impact decisions.

### 2.3 Privacy By Design

We integrate data minimization, access control, and processing boundaries into
product and operational design.

### 2.4 Security By Default

We maintain layered controls to reduce operational and data risks.

### 2.5 Transparency And Explainability

We communicate platform practices clearly and maintain traceable operational
records where feasible.

### 2.6 Compliance And Ethics

We pursue lawful and ethical operations across jurisdictions and sectors where
we work.

## 3. Shared Accountability

Trust is a shared responsibility across Prime Global teams, customers, and end
users. Contracts, policies, and workflows define expected behavior and
escalation paths.

## 4. Governance Practice

Prime Global performs periodic trust and risk reviews that may include policy
updates, control adjustments, incident lessons learned, and improvement plans.

## 5. Reporting Concerns

We encourage reporting of suspected privacy, security, fairness, or ethical
issues through designated channels for triage and resolution.
