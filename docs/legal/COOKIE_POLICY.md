## 1. Purpose

This Cookie Policy explains how Prime Global uses cookies and similar
technologies on our websites and platform interfaces.

## 2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember
actions and preferences over time.

## 3. Technologies We Use

We may use:

- Session cookies for secure navigation.
- Persistent cookies for preference retention.
- Local storage for interface state.
- Tracking pixels and similar tags for performance and analytics.

## 4. Categories Of Cookies

### 4.1 Strictly Necessary

Required for core functionality, including localization, authentication flow
integrity, and security protections.

### 4.2 Functional

Used to remember language and interface preferences.

### 4.3 Analytics

Used to measure usage trends, diagnose service quality, and improve content and
navigation.

### 4.4 Performance And Security

Used to detect abnormal traffic, enforce rate limits, and support incident
response.

## 5. Legal Basis For Cookies

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Where required by law, non-essential cookies are activated only after user
consent. Essential cookies may be set based on legitimate interests or service
necessity.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by service providers supporting infrastructure,
analytics, and communication. These providers process data under contractual and
security controls.

## 7. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage cookies through:

- Platform consent controls where available.
- Browser settings that block or delete cookies.
- Device-level privacy controls.

Disabling essential cookies may impact service availability and security.

## 8. Retention

Cookie duration depends on type. Session cookies expire at browser close.
Persistent cookies remain until expiration or deletion.

## 9. Updates

We may update this policy to reflect legal changes, new technologies, or
platform updates.
