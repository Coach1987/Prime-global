# Prime Global AI Transparency Policy

Effective Date: 2026-07-23  
Last Updated: 2026-07-23  
Version: 0.1 (Draft)

## 1. Policy Objective

This policy explains how Prime Global designs, deploys, monitors, and governs
AI-assisted functionality used in recruitment and business operations.

## 2. AI Use Cases In Scope

AI systems may support:

- Candidate-job relevance scoring.
- Data extraction from structured and unstructured documents.
- Workflow prioritization and recommendation.
- Content moderation and risk flagging in communications.

## 3. Human Oversight Principle

AI outputs are advisory. Prime Global and employer users must apply human
judgment for material decisions, including employment outcomes, compliance
actions, and contract execution.

## 4. Fairness And Bias Controls

We implement controls designed to reduce unfair outcomes, which may include:

- Dataset quality review.
- Model behavior evaluation across protected attributes where lawful.
- Escalation procedures for high-risk or ambiguous outputs.
- Periodic governance review and control recalibration.

## 5. Explainability And Traceability

Where feasible, we maintain records of model versioning, input-output traces,
decision pathways, and human override actions to support accountability,
auditing, and incident response.

## 6. Data Governance For AI

AI-related processing follows privacy, security, and minimization standards,
including controlled access, retention boundaries, and purpose limitation.

## 7. Monitoring And Incident Response

We monitor AI performance for drift, quality degradation, and policy risk.
Material incidents trigger containment, review, remediation, and governance
reporting workflows.

## 8. Prohibited AI Usage

Prime Global does not permit use of AI functions for:

- Automated final hiring decisions without human review.
- Unlawful profiling or discriminatory targeting.
- Processing beyond agreed purposes.

## 9. User Responsibilities

Users of AI-assisted features must:

- Review output confidence and context.
- Validate sensitive recommendations.
- Report suspected errors or harmful outputs.
